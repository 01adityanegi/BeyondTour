Our Story & Vision

Rediscovering the Soul of Devbhoomi

Beyond Tour was born out of a profound love for the Himalayas and a commitment to preserving the living heritage of Uttarakhand. We connect conscious travellers directly with village homestays, verified local mountain guides, indigenous recipes, and age-old artisan guilds.

Our Guiding Pillars

Empowering Himalayan communities while delivering transformative journeys for travellers.

Direct-to-Community Homestays

Eliminating extractive middlemen so that 100% of your stay fees support local Pahadi families, renovate traditional mud-and-slate homes, and stem rural distress migration.

Certified Mountain Guides

Every guide registered on Beyond Tour is a native mountaineer certified in wilderness first aid and local folklore, ensuring both high-altitude safety and rich storytelling.

Preserving Cultural Heritage

From Kumaoni Aipan sacred art to centuries-old culinary secrets like Gahat ki Dal and Bal Mithai, we document and celebrate living rituals before they fade.

Eastern Uttarakhand

Kumaon Region

Characterized by dense oak and rhododendron forests, fruit orchards in Mukteshwar, the spiritual geomagnetic vortex of Kasar Devi, the stone temples of Jageshwar, and the timeless artistry of Aipan geometric patterns.

Western Uttarakhand

Garhwal Region

Home to towering glacial peaks, sacred river confluences (Panch Prayag), revered pilgrimage circuits, the Valley of Flowers biosphere, high alpine bugyals (meadows), and legendary shepherd pathways.

Responsible Mountain Ethics

Travel Light, Leave Only Footprints

We ask every visitor to uphold the sanctity of Himalayan ecology: carry your non-biodegradable waste back to base towns, respect local dress codes at devsthans, support local organic produce, and honor water conservation.
