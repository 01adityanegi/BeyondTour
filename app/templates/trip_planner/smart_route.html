Himalayan Driving Segments & Tips

Segment 1 34 km · ~1.5 hrs

Haldwani → Nainital

Ascends rapidly through the Kathgodam ghats. Start early morning (before 8 AM) to beat tourist peak queues. Consider scenic tea stop by Bhimtal lake or Jeolikote orchards en route.

Elevation climb: 424 m → 2,084 m
Segment 2 65 km · ~2.5 hrs

Nainital → Almora

Scenic ridgeline road via Bhowali pine belt and Khairna bridge over Kosi River. Great stopping point for hot Pahadi Bal Mithai in Almora Lala Bazaar. Wide two-lane mountain highway.

Must try: Singori and Bal Mithai at Almora
Segment 3 205 km · ~7.5 hrs

Almora → Munsiyari

High Kumaon expedition route passing Birthi Falls and Kalamuni Pass (2,740 m). Majestic backdrop of the five Panchachuli peaks. Ensure daylight arrival in Munsiyari valley.

Narrow road in final 35 km; drive with low gear
